## Purpose

These five rules are the IT Help Desk's responsible-use rules. Each rule says
**who** does it, **what** exactly they do, and **when**. Every rule can be
answered with **yes** or **no**, so a technician can check their own work
without asking anyone.

---

## The Five Rules

| # | Area | Rule | Yes / No test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data** | Before pasting a ticket into any AI tool, the agent replaces every name, username, email, device code and phone number with a code such as `User-1`, `Ticket-X` or `Device-1`. | Did I replace all of them before pasting? |
| 2 | **Verification** | Before sending, the agent scores the reply with the five-point verification checklist (Facts · Numbers · No promises · No sensitive data · Complete). Only a reply that scores **5 out of 5** is sent. | Did the reply score 5 out of 5? |
| 3 | **Fairness** | Before a template enters the library, its owner runs the swap test on two fills (for example, formal vs informal, or two invented names) and records that no unexplained difference remains in tone, length, facts or next step. | Is the swap-test result recorded? |
| 4 | **Policy** | The agent uses only AI tools the organization approves. For every reply sent, the template version (for example `IT-Reply-Ticket-v1_2026_10_15`) and the approver's name are recorded. | Is the tool approved, and are version and approver recorded? |
| 5 | **Human decision** | AI never decides access approval, hardware approval or the outcome of a complaint. A person (the employee's manager, the duty technician or the team lead) decides these cases **before** any reply is sent. | Did a person decide before the reply went out? |

---

## How to Use These Rules

- **Every agent** applies Rules 1, 2, 4 and 5 on every reply.
- **The template owner** also applies Rule 3 when adding or updating a template.
- **The reviewer (duty technician)** checks Rules 1, 2 and 5 before signing off.
- **The owner of the facts** is responsible for keeping the IT-Facts block current (version, date and change log).

---

## What Each Rule Protects

| Rule | Protects | Risk it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 – Data | Employees' personal and device data | Data leak, PDPL violation |
| 2 – Verification | Accuracy of every reply | Hallucinations, invented fixes, outdated steps |
| 3 – Fairness | Equal treatment for every employee | Hidden bias in tone, length or next step |
| 4 – Policy | Organization compliance and audit trail | Use of unapproved tools, no accountability |
| 5 – Human decision | People affected by AI outputs | Wrong decisions on access, hardware or complaints |
